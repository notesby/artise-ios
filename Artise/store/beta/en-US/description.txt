Artise is a private, encrypted chat for your family or team, with Ari, an assistant who remembers. Every chat has shared notes, and you can send stickers and make voice and video calls.

To sign in you need an Artise account: ask your workspace's admin for an invite. Then open the app, tap "Sign in manually" and type your workspace's name.

This is a test version of Artise, so it changes often. Tell us what you think: take a screenshot in the app, or open TestFlight, choose Artise and tap Send Beta Feedback.
